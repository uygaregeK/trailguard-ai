# Express Same-Day Istanbul Courier

## §1 Where it is available
Same-day courier delivery is available for addresses inside Istanbul only, on business days.

## §2 Cutoff and cost
Orders placed before **12:00** with the Express option are delivered the same day between 17:00 and 22:00. The Express fee is 149 TRY and is charged even when the order qualifies for free standard shipping.

## §3 Missed deliveries
If the courier cannot deliver, the parcel moves to standard Yurtiçi Kargo delivery the next business day. The Express fee is refunded in that case.

## §4 Race-week tip
Before big races, Express slots fill early. Support cannot reserve a slot or guarantee a delivery time beyond what the checkout shows.
