# Running Club and UTMB Index Partner Perks

## §1 Partner clubs
Members of registered partner running clubs get 10% off full-price items with their club code. The club organizer applies for partnership through the business team.

## §2 UTMB index perk
Runners with a UTMB index can ask for free express shipping (`SHP-002`) on orders placed in the 14 days before a partner race, by sending their runner profile link.

## §3 Bulk orders and sponsorship
Bulk orders for clubs, team kits and race sponsorship are handled by the business team, not by customer support. Support forwards these requests and does not quote bulk prices.

## §4 Combining perks
Partner perks cannot be combined with race-bib codes (`SOP-001`) or with `FINAL-SALE` prices.
