# BOA Dial and Quicklace Replacement

## §1 Scope
Some trail shoes use a BOA dial or a quicklace system (a thin lace with a locking toggle) instead of normal laces, for example the Salomon Speedcross 6.

## §2 Free replacement for the life of the shoe
A broken BOA dial or a snapped BOA lace is replaced free of charge for the life of the shoe, whether or not it is under the 2-year warranty. Replacement kits are shipped free within Türkiye.

## §3 Quicklace
A snapped quicklace or a broken toggle is replaced free within the 2-year warranty in `WAR-001`. After 2 years, replacement kits can be bought separately.

## §4 What the customer sends
The order number, the shoe model and size, and a photo of the broken part.

## §5 Not covered
A dial or lace damaged by cutting, or a shoe that has been modified, is not covered.
