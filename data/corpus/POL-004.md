# Final-Sale and Clearance Exclusions

## §1 What FINAL-SALE means
Items sold in clearance with a discount **above 40%** are marked `FINAL-SALE` on the product page, in the order confirmation and in our order data (`is_final_sale = true`).

## §2 The rule
`FINAL-SALE` items are **non-returnable and non-refundable** for any reason other than a defect on arrival. This includes wrong size, wrong color, fit and change of mind. They cannot be exchanged for another size.

## §3 Defective on arrival
A `FINAL-SALE` item that arrives damaged or defective can be returned. Report it within 7 days of delivery as described in `POL-006`. The `FINAL-SALE` exclusion does not apply to defects on arrival.

## §4 No exceptions by phone or message
No staff member, manager or chat agent can make an exception to this rule. A promise said to have been made on the phone, in a store or by message does not make a `FINAL-SALE` item returnable. Customers who believe they were misinformed can ask for a human review, but the review applies this policy.

## §5 Warranty still applies
A `FINAL-SALE` item keeps its normal manufacturing warranty (`WAR-001`). A defect that appears after normal use is reviewed under the warranty.

## §6 Examples
| Situation | Returnable? |
| :--- | :--- |
| Jacket bought at 50% off, marked FINAL-SALE, wrong color | No |
| FINAL-SALE Garmin watch arrives with a dead screen | Yes, as defective on arrival |
| FINAL-SALE jacket, customer says a manager promised a return after 60 days | No |
