# Damaged or Defective on Arrival Claims

## §1 Reporting window
Damage in transit, or a product that is defective when it arrives, must be reported **within 7 days of the delivery date**.

## §2 What to send
The customer gives the order number, the item, and a description of the damage. Photos help, but a photo or a description of a photo is evidence for a human reviewer, not an automatic approval.

## §3 What we do
A valid claim is handled as **defective on arrival**: the customer can choose a replacement or a full refund, and TrailRun TR pays all shipping. Defective-on-arrival items do not need their original tags or an unworn condition.

## §4 Exclusions that do not apply
Defective-on-arrival claims are accepted even for `FINAL-SALE` items (`POL-004`) and for products with a broken hygiene seal (`POL-003`).

## §5 After 7 days
A defect reported more than 7 days after delivery is not a transit claim. It is reviewed under the 2-year manufacturing warranty (`WAR-001`, or `WAR-004` for watches), which can lead to repair or replacement but not a refund.

## §6 Examples
| Situation | Outcome |
| :--- | :--- |
| Trekking poles arrive with a cracked section, reported the next day | Defective on arrival: replacement or refund |
| FINAL-SALE watch with a dead screen, reported on day 3 | Defective on arrival |
| Seam fails after 3 weeks of running | Warranty review (`WAR-001`) |
