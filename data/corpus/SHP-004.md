# Address Changes and Cancellations After Dispatch

## §1 Before dispatch
While an order has status `PROCESSING`, the customer can change the delivery address or cancel the order free of charge through support.

## §2 After dispatch
Once the parcel is handed to the carrier (status `IN_TRANSIT`), TrailRun TR **cannot change the address or cancel the shipment**. The customer can ask the carrier directly to redirect the parcel to one of its branches using the tracking number.

## §3 Refusing delivery
A customer who no longer wants a dispatched order can refuse delivery. The order is refunded when the parcel returns to our warehouse, minus the original shipping fee.

## §4 Identity checks
Address changes are only made after the customer's identity is checked as described in `SOP-004`.
