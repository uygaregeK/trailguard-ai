# GPS Watch Warranty Routing (Garmin, Coros)

## §1 Separate from WAR-001
GPS watches are **not** covered by the footwear and apparel warranty in `WAR-001`. Watch hardware faults follow the route below.

## §2 Within 7 days of delivery
A watch that is faulty on arrival is a defective-on-arrival claim (`POL-006`): replacement or refund.

## §3 After 7 days: manufacturer warranty
After 7 days, battery, sensor (GPS, heart rate), button and screen faults are handled under the **manufacturer's 2-year warranty**. TrailRun TR passes the claim to the brand's authorized service in Türkiye on the customer's behalf; a specialist checks the watch, and the brand decides on repair or replacement.

## §4 What the customer sends
The order number, the watch model, a description of the fault, and whether the watch has had a software update.

## §5 Not covered
Cracked screens from impact, water damage beyond the rated depth, and normal battery ageing.

## §6 No change-of-mind returns
Paired or activated watches cannot be returned for change of mind (`POL-003`).
