# Size and Color Exchange Rules

## §1 Who can exchange
An item that is eligible for return under `POL-001` (30 days from delivery) and meets its condition rule (`POL-002` for footwear and apparel, `POL-003` for sealed items) can be exchanged for another size or color of the same product. `FINAL-SALE` items cannot be exchanged (`POL-004`).

## §2 Shipping cost of the first exchange
The **first size or color exchange per order is free in both directions**: TrailRun TR pays for the return shipping and for sending the replacement.

## §3 Further exchanges
From the second exchange on the same order, the customer pays the return shipping (a flat 89 TRY with our partner carriers). Sending the replacement remains free.

## §4 Stock
An exchange depends on the replacement size or color being in stock at the time of the request. Support checks current stock in the order system; we do not promise restock dates. If the replacement is out of stock, the customer can choose a refund instead.

## §5 How an exchange works
1. The customer gives the order number and the item.
2. Support checks the delivery date, the item condition rule and the `FINAL-SALE` flag.
3. After the customer confirms, a return request (RMA) is opened with a return shipping code.
4. The replacement ships when the carrier scans the returned parcel.
