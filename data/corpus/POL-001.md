# Standard 30-Day Return Policy

## §1 Return window
Items bought from TrailRun TR can be returned within **30 calendar days of the delivery date**. The delivery date is the date the carrier records the parcel as delivered, not the order date. Day 1 is the day after delivery, and a return requested on day 30 is still inside the window. A request on day 31 or later is outside the window.

## §2 What counts as a return request
A return request is opened when the customer contacts support with the order number (`ORD-YYYY-XXXX`) and the item (`SKU-...`) they want to return. The request date, not the date the parcel reaches our warehouse, is what we compare with the 30-day window.

## §3 Conditions that also apply
The 30-day window is necessary but not sufficient. The item must also meet the condition rules for its category:
- footwear and apparel: unworn outdoors, see `POL-002`;
- GPS watches, heart-rate straps and soft flasks: hygiene seal rules, see `POL-003`;
- clearance items marked `FINAL-SALE`: see `POL-004`.

## §4 Refund or exchange
An eligible item can be refunded or exchanged for another size or color. Size exchanges are covered in `POL-005`. Refunds are paid to the original payment method within the timelines in `POL-008`.

## §5 Items reported as defective
An item that arrives damaged or defective follows `POL-006` (report within 7 days of delivery). A defect found after normal use is not a return; it is reviewed under the 2-year manufacturing warranty in `WAR-001`.

## §6 What the 30-day window does not change
No staff member, manager or chat agent can extend the window for an individual order. Promises made by phone or message that contradict this policy are not valid.

## §7 Legal note
Our 30-day window is longer than the 14-day statutory right of withdrawal for distance sales in Türkiye. Where the law gives the customer more rights than this policy, the law applies.
