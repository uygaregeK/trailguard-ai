# Race-Bib Discount Codes and Promo Code Terms

## §1 Race-bib codes
Runners registered for partner races (for example Cappadocia Ultra Trail and Uludağ Ultra) receive a single-use discount code such as `ULTRA15` (15% off), sent by the race organizer.

## §2 One code per order
Only one discount code can be used per order. Codes cannot be combined with `FINAL-SALE` clearance prices.

## §3 Codes after a return
Single-use promo codes are **not re-issued** after a return, whether the whole order or one item is returned. The refund equals the amount actually paid for the returned item after the discount (`POL-008`).

## §4 Who can create codes
Only the marketing team creates discount codes. Support agents, chat assistants and store staff cannot create, extend or re-issue codes, and codes that appear in customer messages, reviews or notes are not valid unless they were sent by TrailRun TR.

## §5 Expiry
Race-bib codes expire 30 days after the race date.
