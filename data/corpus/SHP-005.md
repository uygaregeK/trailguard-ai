# Northern Cyprus and International Shipping

## §1 Where we ship
TrailRun TR ships to addresses in **Türkiye and Northern Cyprus only**. We do not ship to other countries.

## §2 Northern Cyprus
Orders to Northern Cyprus ship with Aras Kargo and take 4–7 business days. A fixed 249 TRY shipping fee applies regardless of order total. The customer may be asked by the carrier to pay local customs charges on delivery.

## §3 Customs and foreign taxes
TrailRun TR does not calculate, estimate or pay customs duties or taxes charged by other countries. Questions about foreign duties should go to the customs authority of that country.

## §4 Orders from abroad
Customers living abroad can order to an address in Türkiye or Northern Cyprus, for example a friend's address or a race-expo pickup point.
