# Bundles, Gifts and Gift Cards

## §1 Bundles
Some products are sold as a bundle at a lower combined price, for example a hydration vest with two soft flasks. A bundle is returned as a whole. Returning one item from a bundle is possible only if the remaining items are kept at their full individual price, and the difference is deducted from the refund.

## §2 Free gifts
A free gift that came with an order (for example a pair of running socks) must be returned with the item it came with. If it is not returned, its listed value is deducted from the refund.

## §3 Gift cards
TrailRun TR gift cards cannot be returned, refunded or exchanged for cash. A gift card is valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.

## §4 Items paid with a gift card
A refund for an item paid with a gift card goes back to a gift card, not to a bank account (see `POL-008`).

## §5 Gifts sent to someone else
The person who received a gift can return it with the order number. The refund goes to the original payment method of the buyer, not to the recipient.
