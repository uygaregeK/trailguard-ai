# Carbon Trekking Pole Breakage Rules

## §1 Scope
This applies to carbon trekking poles such as the Leki Ultratrail (`PRD-008`).

## §2 Covered
- A section that **cracks or breaks without a fall or impact** during normal use: replaced under the 2-year warranty (`WAR-001`).
- A locking mechanism that fails to hold length.
- A pole that arrives cracked: a defective-on-arrival claim (`POL-006`).

## §3 Not covered
- Breaks from a fall, from being stepped on, or from being used as a lever.
- Tips and baskets worn down by use (sold as spare parts).

## §4 Single-section replacement
Where possible we replace only the broken section, which is faster than replacing the pair.

## §5 What the customer sends
The order number, the pole length (for example 120 cm), photos of the break, and a short description of how it happened.
