# Gore-Tex Waterproof Membrane Warranty

## §1 Scope
This applies to jackets and shoes with a Gore-Tex (GTX) membrane, such as the Salomon Bonatti WP jacket and GTX versions of our trail shoes. It adds detail to the general 2-year warranty in `WAR-001`.

## §2 Covered for 2 years
- **Delamination**: the membrane separating from the outer fabric, seen as bubbles or peeling.
- **Seam leaks**: water coming through taped seams under normal use.

## §3 Not covered
- Abrasion and wear from pack straps or rocks.
- **Rock tears, branch tears and punctures**.
- Loss of water beading on the outer fabric (fixed by re-applying a DWR treatment, see `PRD-007`).
- Damage from washing with fabric softener or tumble-drying on high heat.

## §4 What we do
An approved claim is repaired or replaced; there is no cash refund.

## §5 Examples
| Situation | Covered? |
| :--- | :--- |
| Seams leak after 8 months | Yes |
| Jacket torn on a rock during a race | No |
| Outer fabric no longer beads water | No, needs re-proofing |
