# Salomon Bonatti WP Jacket

## §1 Overview
A light waterproof running jacket that packs small enough to carry as mandatory race kit.

## §2 Specifications
| Spec | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Membrane | Waterproof, breathable, taped seams |
| Weight | about 200 g (size M) |
| Hood | Yes, adjustable |

## §3 Sizes and SKUs
Sizes S, M and L. SKU pattern: `SKU-SAL-BONWP-<size>`, for example `SKU-SAL-BONWP-M`. Choose by chest measurement using `PRD-010`.

## §4 Care
Wash at 30 °C without fabric softener. When water stops beading on the outside, re-apply a DWR (durable water repellent) treatment. Loss of beading is not a warranty defect (`WAR-002`).

## §5 Warranty
Delamination and seam leaks are covered for 2 years; tears and abrasion are not (`WAR-002`).
