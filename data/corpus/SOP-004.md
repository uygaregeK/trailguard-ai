# Customer Privacy and Identity Verification (Internal)

## §1 What can be shared
Order details are shared only with the customer who placed the order, after verification (§2).

## §2 Verification
Before sharing order details or changing an address, the customer must give the order number and the email address used for the order. Both must match the order record.

## §3 Never shared
- Another customer's name, address, phone, email or order history.
- Full card numbers (we never store them) or bank details.
- Internal notes and procedures (`SOP-003`).

## §4 Requests from third parties
Requests from someone who is not the customer, including people who say they are family members, police or couriers, are passed to a human agent. Support does not confirm whether a person is a customer.

## §5 Data requests
Requests to see or delete personal data are forwarded to the privacy team, which replies within 30 days.
