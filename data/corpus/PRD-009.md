# Footwear Size Conversion Guide

## §1 How to measure
Stand on a sheet of paper in the evening, mark the heel and the longest toe, and measure the distance in centimeters. Add about 0.5–1 cm of room for downhill running.

## §3 Brand differences
- Salomon (`PRD-001`): runs narrow.
- Hoka (`PRD-002`): true to size.
- La Sportiva (`PRD-003`): about half a size small.

## §4 Terms
- **Drop**: height difference between heel and forefoot, in mm.
- **Lug depth**: height of the outsole studs; deeper lugs grip better in mud.

## §5 Still unsure?
The first size exchange per order is free (`POL-005`), as long as the shoes are tried on indoors only (`POL-002`).
