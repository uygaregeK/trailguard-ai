# Delayed and Lost Parcel Escalation

## §1 Normal gaps
A parcel can show no new scan for 1–2 business days while it moves between hubs. This is normal and needs no action.

## §2 Escalation after 3 business days
If a parcel has **no carrier scan for more than 3 business days**, support escalates it to the carrier and asks for a trace. The customer is told the case number.

## §3 Declared lost after 7 business days
If a parcel has **no scan for 7 business days**, it is declared lost. TrailRun TR **re-ships the order free of charge**, or refunds it if the item is out of stock. The customer does not have to wait for the carrier's own investigation.

## §4 No other compensation
Apart from the free re-shipment or refund in §3, TrailRun TR does not pay compensation for delays, such as vouchers or cash. Claims against the carrier are handled by TrailRun TR, not by the customer.

## §5 Delivered but not received
If tracking says "delivered" but the customer has not received the parcel, the customer should report it within 48 hours. Support opens a carrier investigation.

## §6 Examples
| Situation | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| No scan for 2 business days | Normal, wait |
| No scan for 4 business days | Escalate to the carrier |
| No scan for 8 business days | Declared lost, free re-ship |
