# Leki Ultratrail Carbon Trekking Poles

## §1 Overview
Light, folding carbon poles for steep climbs in trail races. They fold into three sections and fit in a vest quiver.

## §2 Specifications
| Spec | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Material | Carbon |
| Weight | about 150 g per pole |
| Lengths | 110, 115, 120, 125, 130 cm (fixed length) |

## §3 SKUs
SKU pattern: `SKU-LKI-ULTZ-<length_cm>`, for example `SKU-LKI-ULTZ-120`.

## §4 Choosing a length
A common rule is body height × 0.68. For example, a 175 cm runner usually takes 120 cm.

## §5 Breakage and warranty
Breaks without a fall are covered; breaks from falls are not (`WAR-005`). Poles that arrive cracked are a defective-on-arrival claim (`POL-006`).
