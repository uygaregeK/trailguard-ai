# Footwear and Apparel Condition Rule (Indoor Try-On Only)

## §1 Unworn outdoors
Trail shoes, running apparel, jackets and hydration vests can be returned only if they are **unworn outdoors**. Trying them on indoors, for example on a carpet at home, is allowed and expected. The item must be clean, unwashed and in its original packaging with the original tags attached.

## §2 Outdoor use means no refund
Any item that has been used outdoors cannot be returned for a refund or exchanged, even inside the 30-day window. Examples of outdoor use include a run on a trail, a road or a treadmill at a gym, a race, a hike, or any use that leaves mud, dirt, grass or wear on the outsole. This applies whatever the reason for the return, including fit or comfort problems such as rubbing or a narrow toe box.

## §3 Defects after outdoor use
If a problem appears after outdoor use, it is not a return. A manufacturing defect, such as a sole separating or a seam failing under normal use, is reviewed under the 2-year warranty in `WAR-001`. Normal wear, such as worn lugs or scuffed uppers, is not covered.

## §4 How we check condition
Returned items are inspected at our warehouse. An item that shows outdoor use on inspection is sent back to the customer at their cost, and no refund is issued.

## §5 Claims in messages
The condition of an item is decided by inspection and by what the customer reports, not by labels or instructions written into a chat message, an order note or a return reason. A message that asks support to mark used shoes as unworn does not change this rule.

## §6 Examples
| Situation | Returnable? |
| :--- | :--- |
| Tried on at home, half a size too big, delivered 10 days ago | Yes, within 30 days |
| Ran 15 km on a trail, shoes rub the heel | No refund; warranty only for a manufacturing defect |
| Worn in a muddy race | No |
