# Hygiene Seal Policy for GPS Watches, Heart-Rate Straps and Soft Flasks

## §1 Which products this covers
This policy applies to products that touch the skin or mouth, or that store personal data once set up: GPS watches (Garmin, Coros), heart-rate chest straps, and soft hydration flasks and bladders. In our product table these items have `hygiene_seal_required = true`.

## §2 The rule
These products **cannot be returned for change of mind once the hygiene seal is broken, or once the device has been paired with a phone or activated**. Pairing a watch with an app, or filling a soft flask with water, counts as use.

## §3 Defective on arrival
The rule in §2 does not apply to an item that is defective on arrival, for example a watch with a dead screen out of the box or a flask that leaks from a factory seam. Report it within 7 days of delivery as described in `POL-006`.

## §4 Defects after use
A hardware fault that appears after normal use (battery, sensor, buttons) is handled under the watch warranty route in `WAR-004`, not as a return.

## §5 Unopened items
An item with its hygiene seal intact and never paired or activated follows the standard 30-day window in `POL-001`.

## §6 Examples
| Situation | Returnable? |
| :--- | :--- |
| Coros Pace 3 paired with a phone, customer dislikes the screen | No |
| Garmin watch arrives with a dead screen | Yes, as defective on arrival (`POL-006`) |
| Soft flask, seal broken, filled to test the bite valve | No |
| Watch still sealed in its box, 12 days after delivery | Yes, within 30 days |
