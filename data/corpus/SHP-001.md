# Domestic Shipping, Cutoffs and Free-Shipping Threshold

## §1 Carriers
Orders within Türkiye ship with Yurtiçi Kargo or Aras Kargo. The carrier and tracking number (`TRK-...`) appear in the shipping confirmation email.

## §2 Dispatch cutoff
Orders placed before **14:00 on a business day** are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 14:00, or on weekends and public holidays, are dispatched the next business day.

## §3 Delivery time
Standard delivery takes 1–3 business days after dispatch in most cities, and up to 5 business days for rural areas.

## §4 Shipping cost
| Order total | Standard shipping |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1,500 TRY or more | Free |
| Under 1,500 TRY | 79 TRY |

## §5 Tracking
Tracking updates come from the carrier. Support can see the same scans as the customer; we cannot change a carrier's delivery date.

## §6 Delays
For parcels with no tracking updates, see the escalation windows in `SHP-003`.
