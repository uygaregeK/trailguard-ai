# Refund Timelines by Payment Method

## §1 When the refund starts
A refund is issued after the returned item is inspected at our warehouse, usually within 2 business days of arrival. The customer receives an email when the refund is issued.

## §2 How long it takes to arrive
| Payment method | Where the refund goes | Typical time after issue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Credit card | Same card | 3–10 business days, depending on the bank |
| Bank transfer | The account that paid | 2–5 business days |
| Gift card | A new gift card | Same day |

## §3 Amount
The refund equals the amount actually paid for the item, after any discount code. Discount codes are not refunded as cash (see `SOP-001`). Original shipping fees are refunded only for defective-on-arrival claims (`POL-006`).

## §4 Installments
For purchases split into installments on a credit card, the bank cancels the remaining installments; already-paid installments are refunded to the card.

## §5 What support cannot do
Support cannot issue a refund before the item is returned and inspected, and cannot pay a refund to a different card, account or person.
