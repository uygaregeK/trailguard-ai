# 2-Year Manufacturing Defect Warranty

## §1 What is covered
Footwear, running apparel, hydration vests and trekking poles bought from TrailRun TR carry a **2-year warranty against manufacturing defects**, counted from the delivery date. GPS watches follow a separate route (`WAR-004`).

## §2 Covered vs not covered
| Covered (manufacturing defect) | Not covered (normal wear or damage) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sole separating from the upper under normal use | Worn-down or torn lugs |
| Seam or stitching failing without impact | Abrasion, scuffs, holes from rocks or branches |
| Eyelet or lace loop tearing out early | Damage from washing machines, dryers or heaters |
| Zipper failing on a new jacket or vest | Fit or comfort problems |

## §3 What the warranty gives
An approved claim is repaired or replaced with the same or an equivalent product. The warranty does **not** give a cash refund.

## §4 How to claim
The customer sends the order number, the item, a description of the problem and photos. A specialist reviews the claim within 5 business days and may ask for the item to be sent in.

## §5 Relation to returns
The warranty is not a return. An item used outdoors cannot be returned (`POL-002`), but it can still be reviewed under this warranty if the problem is a manufacturing defect.

## §6 Specific products
Gore-Tex membranes: `WAR-002`. BOA dials and quick laces: `WAR-003`. Carbon trekking poles: `WAR-005`.
